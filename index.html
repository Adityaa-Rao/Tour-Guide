Redefining How the World Experiences Travel

Founded with a passion to break the boundaries of conventional tourism, Travel Dost connects travelers directly with authentic local experiences, bespoke luxury itineraries, and licensed native guides across all seven continents.

  • 25,000+ Multilingual Certified Guides: Experienced historians, mountaineers, and local storytellers who grew up in the valleys.
  • Handpicked Luxury & Boutique Stays: Every property is vetted for comfort, safety, and authentic character with exclusive VIP upgrades.
  • Transparent & All-Inclusive: Zero hidden resort fees or surprise charges — what you see is what you pay.
  • Founded in 2014 by Backpackers: Created to replace crowded tour buses with intimate, tailor-made adventures.
  • 150,000+ Journeys Crafted: Navigating over 80 countries with a verified 99.4% customer satisfaction score.
  • Global Recognition: Crowned World Tourism Excellence Winner for digital traveler safety & guide empowerment.
  • 5% Giveback Initiative: A portion of every booked package directly funds indigenous community schools and wildlife conservation.
  • Zero Single-Use Plastic: Free refillable thermal flasks and eco-trail kits provided on all adventure expeditions.
  • Support Local Artisans: Direct visits to family olive mills, weaving studios, and organic farmers.
  • 100% Refundable Deposit: Cancel up to 7 days before departure with zero penalties or questions asked.
  • Emergency Medical Coverage: Up to $100,000 emergency medical and baggage loss protection included.
  • 24/7 Rapid Concierge Hotline: Instant human support via WhatsApp, phone, and in-app live chat anytime.

Cancellations made between 3 to 6 days prior receive an 80% refund or full credit towards future trips. Zero cancellation fee applies if travel is disrupted by government flight bans or force majeure events.
