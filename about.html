Our Manifesto

Travel is Not a Checklist; It's a Human Connection

In 2014, two passionate backpackers realized that mass tourism had sanitized the world's most breathtaking places into commercial lines and crowded buses. They created Travel Dost ("Dost" meaning true friend or trusted companion) to flip the script.

Today, Travel Dost partners directly with over 25,000 certified native guides, family-run boutique riads, and luxury eco-lodges in 80+ countries. Every dollar spent on Travel Dost directly enriches local indigenous communities and heritage conservation.
