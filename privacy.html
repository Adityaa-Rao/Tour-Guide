Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Travel Dost to research itineraries, communicate with native tour guides, or book accommodations, we collect your name, contact information (email and phone), travel preferences, emergency contact details, and payment billing addresses.

2. How We Protect Your Data

All financial transactions are tokenized via 256-bit bank-grade SSL and processed by certified PCI-DSS Level 1 payment gateways. We never store credit card security codes (CVV) on our servers.

3. Sharing with Tour Guides & Hotels

We only share essential traveler information (name, dates of arrival, dietary restrictions, emergency contact) with your designated verified guides and booked accommodations to ensure your safety and trip execution.

4. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You hold the absolute right to request an export of your stored personal records or demand complete deletion of your account at any time by emailing privacy@traveldost.com.
